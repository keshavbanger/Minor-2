Degree vs. Skill Course

Is a degree still worth it? Or should you just do a 6-month skill course? Let's compare the facts.

Factor 3-Year Graduation Degree 6-Month Skill Course
Duration 3-4 Years (Full depth) 3-6 Months (Fast track)
Cost (Govt College) ₹15k - ₹40k Total ₹30k - ₹1L+ (Private centers)
Govt Job Eligibility Eligible for UPSC, SSC, Bank Not Eligible
Higher Studies Can do MA, MSc, MBA, PhD Limited options
Job Security High (Foundation is strong) Medium (Skill may get outdated)
Social Recognition High (Recognized by society) Variable (Depends on the skill)

The Path of Raju (Skill Only)

Raju did a 6-month Coding Bootcamp after Class 12. He got a job at ₹15k quickly. But 3 years later, when he wanted to apply for a Government exam or an MBA, he couldn't. His growth plateaued because he lacked the foundational degree.

The Path of Meena (Both)

Meena joined B.Sc. CS in a Govt College (fees ₹8k/yr). While studying, she did a 3-month UI/UX course. After 3 years, she had a degree AND a skill. She started at ₹30k and has the freedom to do an MBA or UPSC later.

Hone Your Doubts

Is government college really affordable?

Yes! Most government colleges in MP have fees between ₹3,000 to ₹12,000 per year. With scholarships, it can even be nearly free.

Can I do a skill course DURING graduation?

This is the best path! We recommend pursuing a degree in a government college and using your saved time and money to learn practical skills online or through short-term courses.

Is a degree necessary for government jobs?

Yes, for almost all Grade A and B government jobs (like IAS, IPS, Bank PO, SSC CGL), a graduation degree from a recognized university is mandatory.
