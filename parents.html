Myth vs. Fact

Hover or tap on the cards below to see the reality behind common career misconceptions.

Arts is for students who fail in Science.

THE FACT

Arts leads to roles like IAS Officers, Lawyers, and Economists. It's about interest, not intelligence.

Only Engineering and Medical have high salaries.

THE FACT

Data Scientists, Actuaries (Commerce), and Design Experts often earn more than average doctors/engineers.

Government colleges are lower quality.

THE FACT

Govt colleges have the most experienced faculty and degrees recognized by all central departments.

Listen First

Understand your child's interests before suggesting a stream. Pressuring them leads to burnout.

Don't Compare

Every child is unique. Just because the neighbor's child is an engineer doesn't mean yours should be too.

Explore Costs

A 3-year government degree costs 1/10th of a private skill course and offers much higher job security.
